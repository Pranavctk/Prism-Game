HOW TO PLAY

▲
The 3D Emerald Prism

Rushes continuously forward on its flat base. Each jump rotates the prism across its 3 faces. Your score is the total distance covered!

⚠
Spikes & Hazards

Triangular spikes, pits, and laser cutters destroy you instantly on contact. Jump over or weave around them!

▭
Solid Platforms & Blocks

Land safely on top of blocks on your flat base. Crashing into a block's front wall will shatter the prism!

◎
Jump Pads & Rings

Green jump pads automatically launch you over hazards. Mid-air jump rings give an extra boost when you press jump while touching them!
